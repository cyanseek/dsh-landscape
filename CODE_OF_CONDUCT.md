# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in DSH Landscape welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith while checking evidence, accept correction, give actionable feedback, and focus disagreement on claims and artifacts rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and with reasonable confidentiality.

This policy is compatible with the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
